Privacy policy

Mail Lab · last updated November 21, 2026

Mail Lab is a private Mac app that one person built and uses for their own email. It has no other users, no servers of its own, and no accounts to sign up for.

What Mail Lab accesses

With the owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in screen, Mail Lab accesses the owner's own Gmail account. It uses that access to download, show, search, send, label, move and delete the owner's email, to manage Gmail filters, and to show the owner's calendar.

Where the data goes

Email, calendar data and the Google sign-in are stored only on the owner's own Mac. The sign-in is kept in the Mac's Keychain. Mail Lab has no servers, and it doesn't send Google data anywhere except:

  • back to Google, to carry out what the owner asks (sending, moving, labeling);
  • to the AI service the owner chooses (Anthropic's Claude, under the owner's own account), and only when the owner uses an AI feature. That feature sends the text needed to answer the owner's request.

Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mail Lab's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Mail Lab's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on the Mac deletes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions go to the developer contact listed on Google's sign-in screen for Mail Lab.
